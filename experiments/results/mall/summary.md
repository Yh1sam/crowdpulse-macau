# Mall dataset: person-counting accuracy of the app's detectors

Generated by `experiments/mall_eval.py` from `metrics.json`. Data: Mall dataset (Chen, Loy, Gong & Xiang, BMVC 2012), CUHK, research use only (not redistributed here). 2,000 frames from a shopping-mall webcam, 640x480, 13-53 annotated people per frame (mean 31.2). Detections come from the sensor app's own code run on the frames without re-encoding, at the app's shipped settings.

| detector | share of people counted | count error per frame (MAE) | correlation with true count | boxes that are a person (precision) | people with a box (recall) | recall: near third / middle / far third of image | median latency on Mac |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YOLO11n CoreML (app default) | 59% | 12.9 (bias -12.9) | 0.796 | 93% | 55% (range 53%-55%) | 78% / 64% / 42% | 7.7 ms |
| Apple upper-body (app fallback) | 29% | 22.0 (bias -22.0) | 0.56 | 99% | 29% (range 29%-29%) | 52% / 38% / 18% | 5.8 ms |

By crowd size (annotated people per frame, thirds of the frames):

| detector | smallest third (13-28 people) | middle third (29-34 people) | largest third (35-53 people) |
|---|---|---|---|
| YOLO11n CoreML (app default) | 61% counted, MAE 9.32 | 60% counted, MAE 12.59 | 56% counted, MAE 17.57 |
| Apple upper-body (app fallback) | 30% counted, MAE 16.85 | 30% counted, MAE 21.99 | 29% counted, MAE 28.18 |

**Post-hoc follow-up** (added after seeing the under-count above): per-camera linear calibration, annotated ≈ a × detected + b, fitted on frames 1-800 only and scored on the held-out frames 801-2000.

| detector | raw MAE on frames 801-2000 | calibrated on 20 labelled frames | calibrated on 800 labelled frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| YOLO11n CoreML (app default) | 13.42 | 3.33 (a = 1.6, b = 1.65) | 3.22 (a = 1.423, b = 4.75) |
| Apple upper-body (app fallback) | 23.04 | 4.45 (a = 1.753, b = 14.8) | 4.47 (a = 1.372, b = 17.04) |

Matching rule: head inside box widened by 5% of width per side, from 5% of height above the top to HEAD_FRACTION of height below it; one-to-one assignment by distance to the expected head point; head fraction {'yolo11n': 0.35, 'apple_upper': 0.6} (recall range: ±0.1).
Charts: `mall_count_timeline.png`, `mall_count_scatter.png`. An annotated example frame is written to the git-ignored dataset folder only.
