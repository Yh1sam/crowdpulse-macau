## 3. Who would use it

**Primary user: Public Security Police Force (治安警察局, CPSP/PSP)**
- **Law 14/2018, Art. 3(1)(1) and (5):** "維持公共秩序及安寧" (maintain public order and tranquillity) and "管制及監察車輛與行人的通行" (control and supervise vehicle and pedestrian movement).
- **Art. 3(2):** PSP takes part in civil protection.
- **Art. 6(1)(7):** patrols streets and keeps order during "任何引起公眾聚集的活動" (any activity that draws crowds).
- Source: LA leaflet, https://al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2019-06/738635d00c840e63d2.pdf
- PSP already runs the tiered plan for these zones (Section 1, row 4) and pushes crowd-control notices to hotels and tour guides through the 酒店及旅遊業警務聯絡機制 (Hotel and Tourism Industry Police Liaison Mechanism; GCS, 2026-10-08).
- **Why CrowdPulse fits:** PSP is the body that decides when to start and lift control. Section 1, row 3 shows the timing problem it faces.

**Coordinator: Unitary Police Service (警察總局, SPU)**
- Commands PSP and the Judiciary Police, and coordinates civil protection.
- During holidays it "啟動跨部門聯合行動指揮中心，採取實時聯合指揮" (opens a cross-department joint command centre with real-time joint command).
- Source: Macao Yearbook 2025, GCS, https://yearbook.gcs.gov.mo/yearbook_pdf/2025/myb2025cPA01CH15.pdf
- This is where operator approval of detours would sit (*team inference*).

**Partners (verified roles):**
- **MGTO (旅遊局):**
  - Runs the 114-attraction app.
  - Its border information counters give out crowd-control information.
  - It coordinates with PSP's Traffic Department and DSAT (reply, 2024-11-07, https://www.al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2024-11/6530267346c758b608.pdf).
  - It absorbed the Tourism Crisis Management Office from 2021-02-01 (reply, 2024-11-11, https://al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2024-11/378836744466d9d12d.pdf).
- **IAM (市政署):**
  - Under Law 9/2018, Art. 3 it keeps public places clean and builds and renews public facilities.
  - Its tourist signposts in hotspots carry EasyGo QR codes (reply, 2025-06-10, https://www.al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2025-06/73571685ccfcf32f1a.pdf).
  - It is the natural channel for physical trilingual notices (*team inference*).
- **Cultural Affairs Bureau (文化局, IC):** its official abbreviation is IC, from the Portuguese Instituto Cultural; icm.gov.mo is only its web domain.
  - Runs the Historic Centre Protection and Management Plan, Reg. Adm. 4/2024, in force 2024-06-01 and covering 11 aspects including transportation (GCS, 2024-05-30, https://www.gcs.gov.mo/detail/en/N24EdrsTmg).
  - Its World Heritage Monitoring Centre is "逐步接入…遊客管理及擁擠程度等外部環境數據" (gradually taking in visitor-management and crowding data; reply, 2023-11-03, https://www.al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2023-11/2755465517b3be6a55.pdf).
- **DSAT (交通事務局):** "按照實時交通狀況，動態調整巴士運力" (adjusts bus capacity in real time; reply, 2026-07-10, https://al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2026-07/e12b6eb5a8c7dfd502470e9f9fa6ba08.pdf).
- **SAFP (行政公職局):** "負責統籌、規劃及部署公共部門人工智能應用，並對各部門的AI項目提供安全風險方面的意見" (coordinates public-sector AI and advises departments on its security risks; as stated in a DSPDP reply, 2026-04-21, https://al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2026-04/3801c5b5c3ad78a5e009cb23acb8a160.pdf).
- **Joint operation:** seven departments (DSEDT, IAM, MGTO, PSP, Fire Services, IC, DSAT) jointly ran and reported on the 2026 holiday pedestrian zones (GCS, 2026-10-05, https://www.gcs.gov.mo/news/detail/zh-hant/N26JE8A7oo).

**Added on 13 Oct after a second verification round (each source opened and quoted):**
- **What the police system does today.** Security Secretariat reply to legislator Leong Pou U, 28 Jan 2026 (Portuguese translation; https://www.al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2026-02/0deba43cc8fb4c29b0f91b919a1fde7f.pdf): the system "monitoriza a densidade de pessoas em tempo real através dos dispositivos de sensores" and "emite alerta"; "o CPSP planeia … criando uma previsão da densidade do fluxo de pessoas por zona"; it "está instalado nas zonas entre as Ruínas de São Paulo e o Largo do Senado, e no Posto Fronteiriço das Portas do Cerco (Partida)". ## 5. Medicine

**How a crowd crush kills**
- **Gill JR & Landi K**, *Am J Forensic Med Pathol* 2004;25(4):358–361, doi:10.1097/01.paf.0000147316.62883.8b. Case report of the 1991 crush at a New York City college charity game.
  - "The cause of death of all 9 decedents was asphyxia by chest compression."
  - No fractures; only minimal superficial blunt injuries.
  - Victims "typically die ('standing up') in a vertical position".
  - "When force is applied front to back or vice versa, chest expansion is compromised."
  - The dead "are not just the frail and small of stature".
- **Chang AH et al.**, *Cureus* 2024;16(9):e68811 (editorial), https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11456318/fullTextXML
  - Compressive forces leading to traumatic asphyxiation "are the predominant cause of death during crowd disasters".
  - Itaewon: at least 159 deaths and 196 injuries.
  - Calls for "real-time monitoring of crowd density, early warning systems, and defined triggers". This is the editorial's call; CrowdPulse is one attempt at it (*team inference*).
- **Motomura T et al.**, *J Nippon Med Sch* 2019;86(6):310–321, https://www.nms.ac.jp/sh/jnms/2019/086060310.pdf
  - "Traumatic asphyxia is considered the most common cause of death in human stampede accidents."
- **Kroll MW, Still GK et al.**, *Med Sci Law* 2017;57(2):61–68, doi:10.1177/0025802417695711
  - In crowd crushes, "the fatality mechanism generally does not involve flail chest, but rather prolonged hypoxia from restricted breathing".
- **Fruin 1993** (conference paper, not a journal; https://gkstill.com/Support/crowd-flow/fruin/Fruin2.html)
  - "Virtually all crowd deaths are due to compressive asphyxia and not the 'trampling' reported by the news media."

**Why minutes matter**
- **Motomura 2019:** "respiratory failure can occur within 1 h … even when total thoracoabdominal load is only about 60% of body weight."
- Limits: 14 healthy women in their 20s, lying down. Failure was inferred from a breathing index; no participant actually failed.
- This supports "time at high density matters". It does not test a 5–15-minute horizon (*team inference*).

**Density reference points**

| Value | Source |
|---|---|
| Below 2–3 persons/m² = "normal operation at low risk" (stage 0) | Helbing & Mukerji, *EPJ Data Science* 2012;1:7, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.5856 |
| Above "4 or 5 persons per square meter, congestion can build up quickly … high risks for people to stumble or fall" | G.K. Still's Love Parade expert report, **as summarised by** Helbing & Mukerji 2012 (corrected attribution) |
| 5/m² "should be the UPPER limit for standing/viewing spaces"; 6/m² "High Risk" | G.K. Still, gkstill.com, 2019-02-13 (not peer-reviewed), https://www.gkstill.com/Support/crowd-density/CrowdDensity-1.html and https://www.gkstill.com/Support/crowd-density/CrowdDensity-2.html |
| Maximum 47 persons per 10 m² in standing areas (sports grounds) | Green Guide, 5th ed., 2008, s.2.9 (superseded by the 6th ed. 2018, not checked), https://www.jesip.org.uk/uploads/media/incident_reports_and_inquiries/Guide%20to%20Safety%20at%20Sports%20Grounds.pdf |
| About 7/m²: "the crowd becomes almost a fluid mass" | Fruin 1993 |
| Above 6/m², local flow falls by a factor of 3 or more. Local densities went above 10/m² (with R = 1 m). Local maximum ≈ 2× average ("rule of thumb"). | Helbing et al., *Phys Rev E* 2007;75:046109 (2006 Hajj), https://arxiv.org/pdf/physics/0701203 |

**Global burden:** 215 human stampede events from 1980 to 2007, with 7,069 deaths and at least 14,078 injuries. Source: Hsieh et al., *Disaster Med Public Health Prep* 2009;3(4):217–223.

**At-risk groups (keep the hedges)**
- Motomura 2019 says children and older adults "may" fail sooner. This is an inference; no older adults were tested.
- At Akashi, 9 of the dead were children under 10 and 2 were women in their 70s (Motomura 2019).
- Gill & Landi counter that the dead are "not just the frail".
- A 2026 review (Chowhan et al., *Public Health Rev* 47:1609310) says children, older persons and people with disabilities "face heightened risks". This is background framing with no data.
- Safe line: dense crowds can kill anyone; older, smaller or less mobile people may be more vulnerable.
- How the design uses this: [docs/model-card.md, "Who may be more at risk"](../model-card.md#who-may-be-more-at-risk-fairness-considerations).

## 6. Legal basis and telling the public (to be confirmed with the authority, not a compliance claim)

**Law 8/2005, Personal Data Protection Act** (BO 34/2005, 2005-08-22, https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2005/34/lei08_cn.asp)
- **Art. 4(1)(1):** personal data is any information about an identifiable person, "包括聲音和影像" (including sound and images).
- **Art. 4(1)(3):** "processing" covers any operation, automated or not, including "收集…使用" (collection … use). Running a counting model on camera frames is therefore likely processing even if nothing is stored (*team inference*; ask the authority).
- **Art. 3(3):** the Act covers video surveillance and other capture of images that allow people to be identified. **Art. 3(4)** applies it to public-security processing, without prejudice to special laws such as Law 2/2012.
- **Art. 10(1):** people must be told the controller's identity and the purpose. The statistics/research exemption in Art. 10(5)(3) is unlikely to fit, because signs are cheap (*team inference*).
- **Art. 21(1):** written notification to the authority "從處理開始起八日期限內" (within 8 days of starting).
- **Art. 7(1):** data revealing religious belief is sensitive and prohibited, except with authorisation (**Art. 7(2)(2), Art. 22**). This matters near St Dominic's Church (玫瑰堂). In DSPDP case 0054/2014/IP, filming Macau's streets "無可避免會拍攝到…教堂或廟宇內信徒朝拜的影像資料" (inevitably captures worshippers in churches or temples).

**Law 2/2012, Video Surveillance in Public Places** (BO 12/2012, 2012-03-19, https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2012/12/lei02_cn.asp)
- **Art. 1:** applies only to the security forces and services acting as police. For a non-police operator it is a **template, not a binding duty**.
- **Art. 11(1):** installation needs the Chief Executive's authorisation after a **binding opinion** from the data protection authority. **Art. 11(6):** authorisation lasts at most 2 years and is renewable.
  - The power is delegated to Secretary for Security Chan Tsz King by Executive Order 52/2025, in force 2025-10-16 (https://images.bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2025/41/orde-52-2025.pdf).
- **Art. 12(5):** the application must include "讓公眾知悉設有錄像監視系統的程序" (the procedure for telling the public).
- **Art. 25:** a notice must be posted "在…顯眼處" (in a conspicuous place) saying the system is in use and who is responsible, "應具中、葡文文本及適當的圖示，如具合理理由，可翻譯成英文" (in Chinese and Portuguese with suitable symbols, and in English where justified).
- **Art. 21:** footage kept 60 days at most. **Art. 26:** data may be used for statistics if nobody is identified.
- **Secretary for Security Dispatch 61/2012:** notice models are 60 cm (normal) or 40 cm (reduced) squares in blue, white and red, and must name the responsible entity and its contact (https://images.bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2012/19/despseg-61-2012.pdf).

**The authority**
- **Personal Data Protection Bureau** (個人資料保護局, DSPDP; its English site uses "PDPB"). It became a permanent department on 2024-02-01 (https://www.dspdp.gov.mo/zh_tw/company_profile.html).
- Under **Reg. Adm. 42/2023** it reports to the Chief Executive, has "專屬職責" (exclusive power) to monitor Law 8/2005, and issues the binding opinions under Law 2/2012 (https://bo.dsaj.gov.mo/bo/i/2023/48/regadm42_cn.asp).
- As of June 2026, there is "暫未有針對相關情況發佈具體指引的時間表" (no timetable yet for specific guidance on the situations raised). Source: SAFP reply, 2026-06-16, https://al.gov.mo/uploads/attachment/2026-06/19a2f6a9508fd4e047bc70c59b384a76.pdf

**Precedents to cite carefully**
- **Case 0174/C/2012:** live-view CCTV that records nothing is generally outside the Act, but only if the images are not "以其他方式記錄或處理" (otherwise processed). CrowdPulse processes frames, so this case does **not** exempt it.
- **APPA anonymisation guide** (DSPDP and PCPD Chinese translation, July 2025): "一般而言，經過匿名化處理的資料不會被視為個人資料" (anonymised data is generally not personal data). The guide says it is not legal advice.
- **Case 0025/2010/IP:** layered notices (full, summary, short). The short sign "必須清晰明確，不可遺漏" (must be clear and leave nothing out), so the sign must mention the camera.
- **Case 0014/2014/PA:** blur images or lower their resolution before publication.
- **Permission 06/A/2020/GPDP:** a police face and licence-plate recognition test was allowed with conditions, including no more than 200 cameras and deletion after the test. Its status is now 失效 (lapsed).
- **Reg. Adm. 28/2004, Art. 26(1)(3):** occupying public land for private-interest activities needs a licence. Whether this applies to the phone mounts is for IAM to confirm.

**Responsibility (for discussion, not settled):**
- PSP holds the legal duty for pedestrian movement (Law 14/2018).
- Under Law 8/2005, the department that operates CrowdPulse would be the data controller.
- In the design, a named human operator approves every action and is recorded in the audit log (repo: the dashboard's "Deciding as" field, self-declared with no login; `backend/app/schemas.py` refuses an empty name, "operator" and "system"; see [docs/model-card.md, "Responsibility when it fails"](../model-card.md#responsibility-when-it-fails) and [docs/privacy.md](../privacy.md#the-one-name-the-system-stores-the-person-who-decides)).
